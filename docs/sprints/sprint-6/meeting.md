### Meeting Summary

The team met with the customer to review the deliverables for Sprint 4, focusing on file management and the submission cycle.

**1. Demo: Submissions and Task Descriptions**
The team demonstrated three key new features in the Production environment:
* **Task Descriptions:** The ability for instructors to upload files (e.g., PDFs) attached to specific assignment components.
* **Student Submissions:** Students can now upload their assignment files directly into the system.
* **Download from Gradebook:** Instructors can download student submissions directly from the grading table, streamlining the workflow of "Download -> Review -> Grade."

The customer was satisfied with these features, noting they effectively replace the functionality previously handled via Google Drive and Sheets.

**2. Strategic Plan: Peer Review & Finalization**
The discussion shifted to the final phase of the project.
* **Urgency:** The customer emphasized the need to finish the **Peer Review** part of the system as quickly as possible.
* **Timeline:** The customer wants to finalize the project during the upcoming week.
* **Decision:** The team agreed to dedicate the next sprint exclusively to implementing the Peer Review logic to ensure the project can be finalized on schedule.
